About Bayanlingo

Bayanlingo is a free tool for practicing English the way people actually use it: reading it, writing it, and listening to it — together, not as separate exercises.

Why "Bayanlingo"

The name comes from bayān (بيان), an Arabic word for clear, eloquent expression — paired with "lingo," language. The idea: real fluency isn't just typing speed, it's expressing yourself clearly across reading, writing, and listening together.

What you can do here

  • Practice typing English text generated at your level, from A1 (beginner) to C1 (advanced), across dozens of topics.
  • Listen to any text read aloud, and practice spelling words from audio in Voice Typing Test mode.
  • Look up the Arabic meaning of any word just by hovering over it.
  • Save your own texts to a personal library, organized by level.
  • Track your words-per-minute, accuracy, streak, and earn badges as you improve.
  • Create a free account to keep your progress synced across your devices.

Who it's for

Arabic-speaking learners who want to build real English fluency — not just pass a test — by practicing the language the way it's actually used day to day.
