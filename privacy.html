Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what information Bayanlingo collects, why, and how it is used. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Account data — if you create an account, we store your email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text, and we cannot see it.
  • Practice progress — your typing speed, accuracy, completed lessons, streak, and badges, so your progress can sync across devices when you're logged in.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or track you individually, to understand overall traffic (e.g. how many people visit, which pages are popular).

If you use Bayanlingo without creating an account, your progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and is never sent to us.

How we use it

  • To let you log in and keep your progress synced between devices.
  • To improve the site based on aggregate, anonymous usage patterns.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or third-party marketers.

Where it's stored

Account and progress data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database associated with this project. Cloudflare acts as our infrastructure provider.

Your choices

  • You can use Bayanlingo without an account — nothing about you is sent to our servers in that case.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at bayanlingo12@gmail.com.
